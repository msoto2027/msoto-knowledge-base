# Beginner Workouts

Welcome to the **Beginner Workouts** section of FitIQ!

Whether you're stepping into a gym for the first time or getting back after taking some time away (like me), this section is all about building a strong foundation.

You don't need the perfect workout or the fanciest equipment—you just need a place to start.

Inside this section you'll learn how to create a routine, stay motivated, and measure your progress over time.
