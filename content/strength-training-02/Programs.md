# Programs

A training program is simply a plan for your workouts.

Following a structured program helps ensure you're training all major muscle groups and making steady progress.

## Beginner Recommendation

As someone getting back into the gym, I think a simple full-body routine is one of the best places to start.

Example weekly schedule:

**Monday**

- Full Body

**Wednesday**

- Full Body

**Friday**

- Full Body

This allows plenty of recovery while building strength.

As you become more experienced, you can explore other training splits like:

- Upper/Lower
- Push/Pull/Legs
- Body Part Splits

The best program is the one you can stay consistent with.

## Don't Program Hop

It can be tempting to switch workouts every week because something new looks exciting.

Instead, stick with one program for several weeks before making changes.

Consistency makes it easier to measure your progress.
