# Progress

Getting stronger takes time.

Some weeks you'll notice big improvements.

Other weeks it may feel like nothing is changing.

Both are completely normal.

## Ways to Measure Progress

Progress isn't just about lifting heavier weights.

You might notice that you can:

- Complete more repetitions
- Improve your technique
- Recover faster
- Feel more confident
- Have better endurance
- Lift the same weight more easily

Those are all signs that you're improving.

## Be Patient

Building strength is a long-term process.

The goal isn't to become stronger overnight.

It's to improve a little bit each week.

## Celebrate the Small Wins

Maybe you added five pounds to your squat.

Maybe you finally did your first push-up.

Maybe you simply showed up on a day when you didn't feel like it.

Every step forward counts.
