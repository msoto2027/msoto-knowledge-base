# Exercises

There are hundreds of strength exercises, but beginners don't need to learn all of them right away.

Start by mastering the basic movement patterns.

## Push Exercises

These involve pushing weight away from your body.

Examples:

- Bench Press
- Push-ups
- Shoulder Press
- Chest Press Machine

## Pull Exercises

These involve pulling weight toward your body.

Examples:

- Lat Pulldown
- Seated Row
- Dumbbell Row
- Pull-ups

## Leg Exercises

Strong legs support almost every movement you make.

Common exercises include:

- Squats
- Leg Press
- Lunges
- Romanian Deadlifts
- Leg Curl
- Leg Extension
- Calf Raises

## Core Exercises

A strong core improves balance and stability.

Some beginner-friendly exercises include:

- Planks
- Dead Bugs
- Bird Dogs
- Hanging Knee Raises

## Where to Start

Don't try to learn every exercise at once.

Choose a few basic movements, practice them consistently, and focus on improving your technique.
