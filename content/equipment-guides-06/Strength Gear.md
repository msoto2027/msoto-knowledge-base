# Strength Gear

Strength equipment helps build muscle, increase strength, and improve overall fitness.

You don't need to use every machine—focus on learning the basics first.

## Dumbbells

![[dumbbells.jpg]]

Dumbbells are one of the most versatile tools in the gym.

They can be used for:

- Bench Press
- Shoulder Press
- Lunges
- Rows
- Bicep Curls
- Tricep Extensions

They're great because each side of your body works independently, helping improve balance and coordination.

## Barbells

![[barbells.jpg]]

Barbells are commonly used for larger compound exercises like:

- Squats
- Bench Press
- Deadlifts
- Overhead Press

They're excellent for building overall strength.

## Weight Machines

![[weight machine.jpg]]

Machines guide your movement, making them beginner-friendly.

Common machines include:

- Chest Press
- Leg Press
- Lat Pulldown
- Leg Extension
- Leg Curl
- Seated Row

They're a great way to learn movement patterns before progressing to free weights.

## Cable Machines

![[cable machines.jpg]]

Cable machines provide constant resistance throughout an exercise.

They can be used for nearly every muscle group and are great for both beginners and experienced lifters.
