# Home Setup

You don't need an expensive home gym to make progress.

A few simple pieces of equipment can provide plenty of workout options.

## Beginner Home Gym Essentials

If you're just getting started, consider:

- Resistance bands
- Adjustable dumbbells
- Exercise mat
- Stability ball
- Jump rope

These items don't take up much space and can be used for a wide variety of exercises.

## If You Have More Space

You might eventually add:

- Adjustable bench
- Barbell
- Weight plates
- Squat rack
- Pull-up bar

Remember, you don't need everything at once.

Build your setup over time as your goals and experience grow.

## Home vs. Gym

Both can be great choices.

The best option is the one that helps you stay consistent.
