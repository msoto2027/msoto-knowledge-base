# Endurance

Endurance is your body's ability to keep moving for longer periods without getting overly tired.

The good news is that endurance improves with consistent practice.

## How to Build Endurance

Improve gradually by:

- Increasing workout time
- Exercising consistently
- Challenging yourself a little more each week
- Allowing time for recovery

Small improvements add up over time.

## Don't Rush It

One of the biggest mistakes beginners make is increasing intensity too quickly.

Your heart, lungs, muscles, and joints all need time to adapt.

Listen to your body and increase your workload gradually.
