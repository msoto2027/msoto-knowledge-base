# Basics

Cardio, short for cardiovascular exercise, is any activity that raises your heart rate and gets your body moving.

It strengthens your heart and lungs while improving your body's ability to deliver oxygen to your muscles.

Cardio isn't just for athletes or people trying to lose weight—it's beneficial for everyone.

## Benefits of Cardio

Regular cardio can help you:

- Improve heart health
- Increase endurance
- Burn calories
- Boost energy levels
- Reduce stress
- Improve recovery between workouts
- Support overall health

Even 20–30 minutes a few times a week can make a noticeable difference.

## How Much Cardio Should I Do?

As a beginner, you don't need to overdo it.

Aim for:

- 2–4 cardio sessions each week
- 20–40 minutes per session

As your fitness improves, you can increase the time or intensity.

## Cardio and Strength Training

Cardio and strength training work well together.

Strength training helps you build muscle, while cardio improves your endurance and heart health.

The goal isn't to choose one over the other—it's to find a balance that works for you.
