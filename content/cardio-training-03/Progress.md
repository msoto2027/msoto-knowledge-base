# Progress

Progress in cardio doesn't always mean running farther or faster.

Sometimes it's simply noticing that everyday activities feel easier.

## Signs You're Improving

You might notice:

- You recover faster after exercise.
- You can work out longer.
- Your heart rate returns to normal more quickly.
- Walking upstairs feels easier.
- You're less out of breath during workouts.

These are all great signs of improved cardiovascular fitness.

## Track Your Workouts

Consider keeping track of:

- Time
- Distance
- Pace
- Heart rate
- How you felt after the workout

Looking back after a few months can be incredibly motivating.

## Stay Consistent

Like strength training, cardio progress takes time.

Focus on building a routine you enjoy rather than chasing quick results.

Remember, every workout is another step toward a healthier lifestyle.
