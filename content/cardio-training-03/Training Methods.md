# Training Methods

Not all cardio workouts are the same.

Different training methods can help you reach different goals.

## Steady-State Cardio

This means maintaining a comfortable pace for a longer period of time.

Examples:

- Walking
- Jogging
- Cycling
- Swimming

Steady-state cardio is great for beginners because it's easy to control your pace.

## Interval Training

Interval training alternates between periods of higher effort and easier recovery.

Example:

- Walk for 2 minutes
- Jog for 1 minute
- Repeat

Intervals can improve endurance while making workouts more interesting.

## High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT)

HIIT involves short bursts of intense effort followed by recovery periods.

These workouts are usually shorter but more challenging.

If you're just getting back into fitness, it's okay to wait until you've built a solid foundation before trying HIIT.

## Which Method Is Best?

There isn't one "best" method.

Choose the style that matches your goals and current fitness level.
