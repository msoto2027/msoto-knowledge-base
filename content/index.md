# FitIQ Knowledge Base

> A personal fitness and wellness resource designed to help me learn, improve, and build healthier habits.

## About FitIQ

FitIQ Knowledge Base is a comprehensive fitness resource created to help individuals achieve their fitness and wellness goals.

This knowledge base covers important areas of fitness, including:

- Workout routines
- Strength training
- Cardiovascular exercise
- Nutrition
- Injury prevention
- Fitness equipment

Whether someone is beginning their fitness journey or looking to improve their current training, FitIQ provides organized and easy-to-understand information to support continued learning and progress.

## Why I Created FitIQ

Fitness is something that is personally important to me.

My brother and I recently decided to restart our fitness journey together before he leaves for Texas Tech this fall. Creating this knowledge base gives me an opportunity to learn more about:

- Exercise
- Nutrition
- Healthy habits
- Training strategies
- Long-term fitness improvement

Because this project connects to my own goals and experiences, I believe it will help me stay engaged while creating a resource that can also support others working toward similar goals.

## Fitness Journey

My goal with FitIQ is not just to collect information.

It is to build a place where I can:

- Learn the fundamentals of fitness
- Track my progress
- Understand how my body improves
- Develop healthier habits
- Continue improving over time

Fitness is a lifelong process, and learning is part of the journey.

Progress comes from consistency, learning, and building habits over time.

I do not need to be perfect.

I need to keep learning, improving, and creating a routine that supports my goals.
