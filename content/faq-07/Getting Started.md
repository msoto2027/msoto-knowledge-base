# Getting Started

## I'm completely new. Where should I begin?

Start with the basics and don't try to learn everything at once.

Begin here:

- [[Beginner-workouts-01/Getting Started|Beginner Workouts]]
- [[Beginner-workouts-01/Creating First Routine|Creating Your First Routine]]

---

## How many days should I work out?

A good starting point is **3–4 days per week**.

That gives your body time to recover while helping you build a consistent habit.

Learn more in [[Beginner-workouts-01/Staying Consistent|Staying Consistent]].

---

## How long should my workouts be?

Most beginner workouts can last **45–60 minutes**.

Quality is more important than spending hours in the gym.

---

## What if I don't know how to use the equipment?

Everyone starts somewhere.

Don't hesitate to ask a gym employee or trainer for help.

You can also learn more in:

- [[Equipment-guides-06/Basics|Equipment Guides]]
- [[Equipment-guides-06/Strength Gear|Strength Gear]]

---

## Is it okay to start small?

Absolutely.

Starting small and staying consistent is much better than doing too much and burning out.
