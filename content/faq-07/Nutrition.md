# Nutrition

## Do I need to follow a strict diet?

No.

Healthy eating is about consistency, not perfection.

Learn more in [[Nutrition-04/Basics|Nutrition]].

---

## How much protein should I eat?

Your protein needs depend on your body size and fitness goals, but making protein part of each meal is a great place to start.

See [[Nutrition-04/Macronutrients|Macronutrients]].

---

## Are carbohydrates bad?

Not at all.

Carbohydrates provide energy for your workouts and daily activities.

---

## Do I need supplements?

Most people don't.

Good nutrition, sleep, and consistent training should always come first.

Learn more in [[Nutrition-04/Supplements|Supplements]].

---

## Can I still enjoy my favorite foods?

Absolutely.

Fitness is about balance.

Enjoying treats occasionally won't ruin your progress.
