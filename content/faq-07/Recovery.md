# Recovery

## Why are rest days important?

Your muscles recover and adapt during rest.

Recovery is part of getting stronger.

---

## Should I stretch before working out?

A dynamic warm-up is usually best before exercise.

Longer, static stretching is often better after your workout.

Learn more in [[Injury-prevention-05/Warm Up|Warm Up]].

---

## How much sleep do I need?

Most adults should aim for **7–9 hours** of sleep each night.

Good sleep supports recovery and overall health.

---

## Why am I always sore?

Your body may still be adapting to training.

Recovery, nutrition, hydration, and sleep all play important roles.

---

## Should I work out if I'm sore?

Mild soreness is usually okay.

If you're experiencing significant pain or your muscles haven't recovered, consider taking a rest day or working a different muscle group.
