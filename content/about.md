# About Me

> Learning, growing, and building healthier habits through fitness and wellness.

## Hello!

My name is Marisol, and I am a senior at Texas Tech University with a passion for fitness, music, reading, and discovering new experiences.

I enjoy staying active, learning about health and wellness, attending concerts, and spending time with my family and friends. I am always looking for opportunities to grow, challenge myself, and develop habits that contribute to a balanced and healthy lifestyle.

## My Fitness Journey

Fitness has become an important part of my life because it represents growth, consistency, and personal improvement.

Recently, my brother and I decided to restart our fitness journey together before he leaves for Texas Tech this fall. This experience encouraged me to learn more about exercise, nutrition, recovery, and healthy habits.

Instead of only focusing on workouts, I wanted to better understand the different parts of fitness and how they work together.

## Why I Created FitIQ

My interest in fitness inspired me to create **FitIQ Knowledge Base**, a resource designed to provide reliable and easy-to-understand information for individuals at different stages of their fitness journey.

Through FitIQ, I am able to:

- Learn more about exercise and wellness
- Organize important fitness information
- Build better habits
- Create a resource that can help others

This project allows me to combine my personal interest in fitness with my desire to continue learning and sharing knowledge.

## My Goals With FitIQ

My goal is to create a resource that helps people feel more confident when starting or continuing their fitness journey.

I want FitIQ to encourage people to:

- Learn the basics of fitness
- Create sustainable habits
- Understand the importance of nutrition and recovery
- Find an approach that works for their lifestyle

Fitness is not about being perfect.

It is about continuing to learn, improve, and make progress over time.

## Beyond Fitness

Outside of fitness, I enjoy:

- Listening to music
- Attending concerts
- Reading
- Spending time with family and friends
- Exploring new experiences

These interests remind me of the importance of balance and finding activities that bring enjoyment into everyday life.

## What I Hope To Learn

Through creating FitIQ, I hope to continue developing my understanding of:

- Exercise science
- Nutrition
- Wellness
- Healthy lifestyle choices

More importantly, I hope to build a resource that reflects my own journey while helping others work toward their goals.

## Thank You For Visiting FitIQ

Thank you for taking the time to explore FitIQ Knowledge Base.

I hope this resource helps make fitness information easier to understand and encourages others to continue learning and improving along their own journey.
