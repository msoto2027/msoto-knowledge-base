# Supplements

Supplements can be helpful, but they're exactly what their name suggests—a supplement to a healthy diet.

They shouldn't replace nutritious food or consistent training.

## Do You Need Supplements?

Not necessarily.

Most people can make excellent progress with:

- Good nutrition
- Regular exercise
- Quality sleep
- Proper recovery

Supplements can fill gaps, but they aren't magic.

## Common Supplements

### Protein Powder

![[Protein_supplement.pdf]]

A convenient way to increase your protein intake if you're struggling to meet your daily needs through food.

### Creatine Monohydrate

One of the most researched supplements available.

It may help improve strength and support muscle growth when combined with resistance training.

### Multivitamins

These can help if your diet is missing certain vitamins or minerals, but they shouldn't replace a balanced diet.

## Remember

Supplements support your progress—they don't create it.

Focus on building healthy habits first.
