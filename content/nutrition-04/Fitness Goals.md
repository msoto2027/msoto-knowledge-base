# Fitness Goals

> One thing I'm learning is that there isn't a single "best" way to eat. The way I approach nutrition depends on what I'm trying to achieve.

It's easy to compare my diet to someone else's, but everyone has different goals. Someone training for a marathon probably won't eat the same way as someone trying to build muscle, and that's okay.

The important thing is understanding my goal and making small changes that support it.

## Losing Weight

If my goal is to lose weight, nutrition plays a big role.

Some habits I'm focusing on include:

- Eating balanced meals
- Including plenty of protein
- Eating more fruits and vegetables
- Drinking enough water
- Paying attention to portion sizes

I'm trying to avoid crash diets because they're difficult to maintain and usually aren't a long-term solution.

## Building Muscle

If my goal is to build muscle, I need to support my workouts with enough food and protein.

Some things I'm keeping in mind:

- Eat enough protein throughout the day
- Don't be afraid of carbohydrates
- Stay consistent with [[strength-training-02/index|Strength Training]]
- Prioritize recovery

Building muscle takes time, so I'm reminding myself to be patient.

## Maintaining My Weight

Not every fitness goal involves gaining or losing weight.

Sometimes the goal is simply to:

- Feel healthier
- Have more energy
- Improve performance
- Build strength
- Stay active

Nutrition can support all of those goals without needing to make major changes.

## Body Recomposition

This is something I recently learned about.

Body recomposition means trying to build muscle while reducing body fat over time.

It usually involves:

- Consistent strength training
- Eating enough protein
- Getting enough sleep
- Being patient with progress

Changes often happen gradually, so I'm trying not to focus only on the number on the scale.

## Choosing My Goal

It's okay if my goal changes over time.

Right now, I'm asking myself:

- What do I want to improve?
- What feels realistic?
- What habits can I stick with?

The best goal is one that keeps me motivated without feeling overwhelming.

## 💡 What I'm Taking Away

I don't need to chase someone else's goals.

As long as I'm eating in a way that supports **my** goals and building habits I can maintain, I'm moving in the right direction.
