# Recovery

Recovery is where your body repairs itself after training.

Without proper recovery, it becomes harder to make progress and easier to become overly tired or injured.

Recovery is an important part of every fitness routine.

## Prioritize Sleep

![[sleeping.jpg]]

Sleep is one of the best recovery tools available.

Aim for consistent, quality sleep whenever possible.

Your body uses this time to repair muscles and recover from exercise.

## Stay Hydrated

![[hydration.jpg]]

Water supports nearly every function in your body.

Staying hydrated can help with:

- Performance
- Recovery
- Energy levels
- Overall health

## Eat Well

Your body needs nutrients to recover after workouts.

A balanced diet with enough protein, carbohydrates, and healthy fats can support muscle repair and energy levels.

Learn more in [[Nutrition-04/Basics|Nutrition]].

## Take Rest Days

Rest days aren't a sign of weakness.

They're part of the process.

Giving your muscles time to recover allows you to come back stronger for your next workout.

## Active Recovery

Recovery doesn't always mean sitting still.

Light activities like:

- Walking
- Stretching
- Yoga
- Easy cycling

can help you stay active while giving your body a break.
