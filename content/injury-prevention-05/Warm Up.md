# Warm Up

A good warm-up prepares your body for exercise and can make your workouts feel better from the very first set.

It doesn't have to be long or complicated.

Even 5–10 minutes can make a difference.

## Benefits of Warming Up

A proper warm-up can:

- Increase blood flow
- Raise your body temperature
- Improve mobility
- Prepare your muscles for exercise
- Help you mentally focus on your workout

## Simple Warm-Up Routine

Before strength training, you might:

- Walk on a treadmill for 5 minutes
- Do arm circles
- Perform leg swings
- Bodyweight squats
- Light stretches or mobility exercises
- Warm-up sets with lighter weights

The goal is to prepare your body, not tire yourself out.

## Before Cardio

If you're planning a run or bike ride, start with a slower pace before increasing your intensity.

Giving your body a few minutes to adjust can help you feel more comfortable throughout your workout.
