# Safe Training

Training hard is important, but training safely helps you stay consistent over the long term.

You don't have to push yourself to the limit every workout.

## Focus on Proper Form

Before increasing weight, make sure you can perform the exercise with good technique.

Good form helps:

- Target the right muscles
- Reduce unnecessary strain
- Build confidence
- Prevent injuries

If you're unsure about an exercise, it's okay to ask for help or spend time practicing with lighter weights.

## Increase Gradually

One of the biggest causes of injuries is doing too much too soon.

Instead:

- Increase weight gradually
- Add reps slowly
- Build endurance over time

Your body needs time to adapt.

## Listen to Your Body

It's normal to feel challenged during a workout.

It's not normal to ignore sharp or sudden pain.

Knowing the difference between muscle fatigue and pain is an important part of training safely.
